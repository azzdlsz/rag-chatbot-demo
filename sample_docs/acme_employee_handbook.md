# Acme Corp Employee Handbook

## Working Hours
Standard working hours are 9:00 to 18:00, Monday to Friday. Employees may work remotely up to three days per week with manager approval.

## Paid Time Off
Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid annual leave per year. Unused leave of up to 5 days can be carried over to the next year. Sick leave is unlimited but requires a doctor's note after 3 consecutive days.

## Equipment
Every new employee receives a laptop, a monitor and a $300 home-office allowance during their first month.

## Expenses
Business expenses must be submitted within 30 days through the finance portal. Meals during business travel are reimbursed up to $50 per day.
